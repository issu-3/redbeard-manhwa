## 2024-05-24 - Accessibility for Reader Actions
**Learning:** Icon-only actions in reader interfaces (like bookmark, comment, or settings buttons) often lack text labels, making them inaccessible to screen readers and potentially unclear to new users.
**Action:** Always provide dual accessibility features: `aria-label` for screen reader users and `title` (tooltip) for mouse users on all icon-only buttons to ensure they are fully usable and understandable by everyone.
